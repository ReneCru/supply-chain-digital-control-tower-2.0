# Public Data Policy

Only synthetic demonstration data belongs in this recruiter-facing repository.

Do not commit:

- employer exports;
- supplier/customer names;
- real purchase orders;
- part numbers or contract identifiers;
- ERP screenshots or extracts;
- emails or personal information;
- credentials or tokens;
- internal process documents;
- production configuration.

Public sample data should use obvious synthetic identifiers such as `SYN-SUP-*` and `SYN-PO-*` and exist only to demonstrate the engineering workflow.
