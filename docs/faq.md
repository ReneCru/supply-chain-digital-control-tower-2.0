# Portfolio FAQ

### Is this the complete application?
No. This is a curated Portfolio Edition. The complete development edition remains private.

### Is the data real?
No. Public demonstration data and identifiers are synthetic.

### Is this an autonomous procurement agent?
No. Material action requires an explicit human decision in the demonstrated workflow.

### Does the +12 percentage-point example prove the action worked?
No. It demonstrates outcome measurement. The project deliberately avoids converting temporal association into an unsupported causal claim.

### Why publish any code at all?
The public vertical slice makes the engineering claims inspectable: business contracts, deterministic KPI logic, workflow governance, SQL persistence design, tests, and CI can be independently reviewed.

### Why not publish the full intelligence engine?
The complete policy, intelligence, recommendation, remediation, provenance, and predictive implementation contains differentiated design that is not necessary for recruiter evaluation.
